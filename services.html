From assessment to ongoing support

We start by mapping a workflow with your team: its inputs, tools, exceptions, and owner. We agree what the pilot should deliver and which actions need approval, then implement it in your environment. After testing, we help your team adopt it and provide maintenance and optimisation under the agreed engagement.

Questions before you start

Do you work with businesses outside the UAE?

Yes. Utana is based in Dubai and works with businesses worldwide. We agree communication, access, and handover arrangements around your team.

Can we keep our existing tools?

We assess your current systems first. Available APIs, account permissions, and the workflow determine what can be connected. Where appropriate, we can also assess computer-based workflows with sapiens4.

How is the work priced?

Scope and subscription terms are agreed after reviewing your workflow. Software subscriptions, model usage, and third-party services are considered separately so you can assess the ongoing cost.

Who controls access and approvals?

Your business controls its accounts, data access, and workflow permissions. We agree approval points for publishing, customer commitments, spending, and other consequential actions.
