sapiens4: agents that work on your computer

Source-available software for macOS and Windows, with local agents and computer access for business process automation.

Bring agents into the tools you already use

sapiens4 runs locally and combines conversational agents, shared tasks, notes, and computer access. With the permissions you grant, agents can work with applications and information available on that computer. Utana helps turn a suitable business process into a scoped workflow with clear review points.

macOS and Windows support

The project documents macOS 13+ and Windows 10/11 support, including Windows ARM64 and x64 desktop packages. Setup requirements differ by platform. Follow the current project instructions for installation, permissions, and Codex access.

Free to use within the community license

sapiens4 is source-available under the Sustainable Use License 1.0. The project permits personal, non-commercial, and your own internal business use under its terms. This is not an unrestricted open-source license. Model usage, third-party subscriptions, and consulting may carry separate costs.
