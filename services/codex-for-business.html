Codex for marketing and solopreneurs

Build repeatable ways to research, prepare, review, and improve your work with Codex.

Codex for marketing teams

We help teams define useful tasks and prepare the context, files, and connected tools needed to carry them out. Example starting points include reviewing campaign exports, preparing content from an approved brief, updating a landing page, or building a repeatable reporting workflow. Available actions depend on the tools and access configured for the engagement.

Codex for solopreneurs

When one person handles delivery, marketing, and administration, scattered context creates repeated work. We help organise a workspace around clear briefs, reusable instructions, reference material, and review steps. Start with a task you already understand so you can judge the output and correct the process.

A practical adoption process

We select a workflow, define its input and expected output, set up the permitted tools, and test it against examples from your work. The handover explains how to run the workflow, review results, and handle exceptions. Ongoing support can refine it as your needs change.

Keep ownership of the result

Marketing claims, customer messages, account changes, and spending need the review you agree with your team. We make those checkpoints part of the workflow. Codex access and any paid software or model usage are separate from Utana’s consulting and support.
