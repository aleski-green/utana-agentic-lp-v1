Marketing and sales automation

Connect campaign activity to conversations, qualified enquiries, and the next sales action.

Keep the journey from advert to enquiry connected

Marketing automation is most useful when the next team can act on the result. We help connect campaign briefs, approved content, incoming enquiries, and CRM updates so your sales team has the context it needs. A pilot can focus on one channel or one repeated handoff before expanding.

WhatsApp automation with a clear handover

We help design WhatsApp workflows that collect missing enquiry details, route conversations, prepare replies, and keep sales records current. The implementation depends on your WhatsApp Business setup, available integrations, and messaging permissions. People remain available for exceptions and sensitive conversations.

Meta ads optimisation based on enquiry quality

We connect campaign reporting with the outcomes your team records: qualified leads, booked calls, and sales progress. An agentic workflow can identify patterns and prepare tests for messaging, creative, or audience settings. Your team approves budget, targeting, and publishing changes.

Sales automation beyond data entry

Start with enquiry qualification, CRM hygiene, proposal preparation, or follow-up drafts. Define which facts must be verified and which commitments require a person. Missing data should create a review task rather than an invented answer.

What we deliver and measure

The agreed scope can include workflow mapping, integration setup, review steps, reporting, and a handover for your team. Compare response time, manual updates, qualified enquiry rate, and cost per qualified enquiry against your starting point. These are measurement criteria, not promised results.
