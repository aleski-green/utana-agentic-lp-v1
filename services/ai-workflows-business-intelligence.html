Agentic workflows and business intelligence

Turn operational data into a clear picture of what happened, what needs attention, and what to do next.

Connect the work behind the report

Business intelligence depends on consistent definitions and reliable inputs. We help connect spreadsheets, CRM records, operational systems, and documents around a specific decision. Agree the metric, source, reporting period, and owner before automating the output.

Give each agent a bounded task

An agentic workflow can separate collection, validation, analysis, and review. One step gathers permitted data; another checks completeness; another prepares an explanation with source references. Exceptions go to a person instead of being hidden inside a finished report.

Start with a decision your team makes regularly

Possible starting points include weekly sales reviews, campaign performance reports, invoice exceptions, procurement follow-up, and reporting across locations. We choose a scope that can be tested against your current process and make the result available to the people who need it.

Check accuracy as well as time saved

Measure reconciliation errors, time spent preparing a report, unresolved exceptions, and the ability to trace a conclusion to its source. Human review stays in place for financial commitments, customer promises, and changes to operational policy.
