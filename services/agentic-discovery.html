Agentic discovery for your business

Help people and AI assistants find your business, understand what you offer, and assess whether you fit.

Make your services easy to find and understand

Agentic discovery is how a business is found and assessed through AI-assisted research. We work on the public information an assistant or search engine can retrieve: service pages, business identity, useful examples, and clear next steps.

Start with crawlable, consistent information

We review page accessibility, indexing controls, canonical URLs, sitemaps, internal links, and structured data. Service descriptions should agree with visible company details and external profiles. AI search crawler access is reviewed separately from preferences about model-training crawlers.

Give buyers useful evidence

We help turn your actual experience into service explanations, answers to buyer questions, and project write-ups. A useful page explains who the service is for, what the engagement includes, what it needs from the client, and how results are assessed. Any claims about outcomes need supporting evidence.

Measure discovery and qualified enquiries

We can help set up Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools with your access. Review indexing, relevant queries, cited pages where reporting is available, and enquiries that match your services. Citation counts are different from visits, and neither alone proves a business outcome.

What this service does not promise

No provider can guarantee a search position or inclusion in an AI answer. An llms.txt directory may be useful to tools that consume it, but it is not a substitute for accessible pages or useful content.

Reference: Google’s guidance on generative AI search and OpenAI’s crawler documentation.
