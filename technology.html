Technology chosen around your work

We help you assess, configure, and improve the tools that fit your workflow, access requirements, and budget.

Codex, Kimi, DeepSeek, and Gemini

Codex is our primary tool for building workflow automation. We also help with Kimi, DeepSeek, and Gemini as part of an agreed implementation or support engagement. Model and product selection depends on the task, account availability, data requirements, and operating cost.

OpenClaw, Hermes, grokbot, and Muse

We also help businesses working with OpenClaw, Hermes, grokbot, and Muse. Bring the product or repository you use and the workflow you want to improve. We confirm the version, permissions, integrations, and support scope before implementing changes.

sapiens4 for computer-based workflows

sapiens4 runs on macOS and Windows and combines local agents with computer access. Utana can help assess its fit, configure a workflow, and support adoption within the project’s license terms.

Our model policy

We do not use Claude models in business workflows. This applies to Utana’s client implementations and model selection. We help clients choose alternatives that fit their workflow and operating requirements.

Scope before integration

We check what each tool can actually access and do in your environment. Third-party subscriptions, model usage, availability, and terms remain separate from our service. Our support does not imply a partnership or certification from the vendors named here.
